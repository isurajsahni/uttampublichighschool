Academics

A Curriculum Built on Understanding, Not Memorising

Uttam Public High School follows the Punjab School Education Board curriculum, taught in English medium from the earliest years. Our academic programme is designed to move students steadily from guided learning in the pre-primary years to independent, exam-ready study by Class X.

Rather than treating marks as the only measure of progress, our teachers focus on whether a concept has genuinely been understood. Lessons combine textbook work with discussion, practical activity, and regular revision, so that students build knowledge that lasts well beyond the examination hall.

Our Academic Programmes

Every stage has its own focus, so that each year builds naturally on the one before it.

Pre-Primary

Nursery – UKG

A gentle first step into school. Children learn letters, numbers, colours and rhymes through play, storytelling and activity, while settling into routine and making their first friendships.

Primary

Class I – V

The foundation years. Strong reading, writing and number skills are built across English, Punjabi, Hindi, Mathematics and Environmental Studies, with plenty of encouragement along the way.

Middle School

Class VI – VIII

Subjects deepen and separate. Students take up Science, Social Studies and Computer Science in earnest, and begin practical work, projects and structured note-making.

Secondary

Class IX – X

Board preparation with proper support. Regular practice papers, doubt-clearing sessions and individual attention help every student walk into the PSEB examinations well prepared.

How We Teach

Experienced teachers, modern classrooms, and the time to make sure nobody is left behind.

Smart Classrooms

Digital boards bring diagrams, experiments and historical events to life. Seeing a concept demonstrated makes it far easier to remember than reading about it alone.

Individual Attention

Teachers track each student's progress and step in early when something is not clicking. Extra help before school and during free periods is offered as a matter of routine.

Learning by Doing

Laboratory practicals, model-making, group projects and our annual science exhibition give students the chance to test ideas with their own hands.

Assessment

How Progress Is Measured

Assessment at Uttam Public High School is continuous rather than concentrated into a single stressful week. Parents always know where their child stands and what the next step is.

Talk to Us
  • Weekly class tests keep revision steady and highlight gaps while there is still time to fix them.
  • Term examinations follow the PSEB pattern, so board exams feel familiar rather than daunting.
  • Project and practical work is assessed alongside written papers, rewarding effort and creativity.
  • Parent teacher meetings are held regularly, with honest feedback rather than formalities.
  • Pre-board practice papers in Class X build exam temperament, timing and confidence.
