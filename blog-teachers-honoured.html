Uttam Public School Teachers Honoured for Excellence in Education

Published 12 March 2026

The school auditorium was full well before the programme began. Students had saved seats for their class teachers, parents arrived with flowers, and the senior wing choir practised in the corridor. The occasion was a special felicitation ceremony held at Uttam Public School to honour our teachers for their excellence, commitment, and outstanding contribution to quality education.

Teachers from the primary, middle, and senior wings were called to the stage one by one and presented with a certificate of appreciation and a memento. For many of them, the loudest applause did not come from the guests, but from the rows of students who have sat in their classrooms for years.

Established in 2004 in Ludhiana, Punjab, Uttam Public School began with a small building, a handful of classrooms, and a simple belief that good teaching changes lives. More than two decades later, the school serves over 1500 students with a faculty of 50+ teachers. Almost everything the school has become can be traced back to the people standing on that stage.

What Our Teachers Were Recognised For

The felicitation was not based on seniority alone. Over the academic year, the management reviewed classroom practice, feedback from students and parents, and the steady record of academic performance across sections. A few themes came up again and again:

  • Personalised attention in every classroom, from knowing each child by name to identifying exactly where a student is struggling and arranging quiet remedial support before or after school hours.
  • Confident use of our smart classrooms and modern teaching methods, including digital boards, visual explanations, and activity-based lessons that make difficult concepts easier to picture.
  • Mentoring students well beyond the syllabus, helping them manage exam pressure, think about career choices, speak up in front of an audience, and take part in sports, debates, and cultural programmes.
  • Consistently strong board results, with the school maintaining its 98% results through steady preparation, practice papers, and doubt-clearing sessions rather than last-minute pressure.
  • Genuine support for one another, with experienced teachers guiding newer colleagues, sharing lesson plans, and stepping in whenever a class needs cover.

Some of the most moving moments came from the primary wing, where teachers patiently guide five and six year olds through their first words and first friendships. Equally appreciated were the senior teachers who give up their Saturdays during board season, sitting with small groups of students until a chapter finally makes sense.

How the School Supports Its Teachers

Recognition of this kind cannot be a single afternoon in the calendar. It has to be backed by year-round investment in the people who teach. Every new teacher who joins Uttam Public School goes through a structured induction that covers our academic approach, classroom expectations, and the way we communicate with parents.

Through the year, the school runs in-house training sessions and subject workshops on lesson planning, assessment design, child psychology, and effective use of digital teaching tools. Teachers also meet in weekly subject-wise groups for collaborative planning, where they map out upcoming units together, compare what worked in different sections, and prepare shared question banks and activity sheets. Peer observation is encouraged rather than feared, and teachers are supported in attending external workshops and board orientation programmes so that classroom practice keeps improving.

A Message from the Principal

Addressing the gathering, the Principal thanked the faculty for the quiet, everyday work that rarely makes it into a report. A school, the Principal said, is not its building or its notice boards; it is the teacher who stays back for one more explanation, the teacher who notices a child has stopped smiling, and the teacher who reads answer sheets late into the evening so that feedback reaches students the next morning. Every result the school celebrates belongs first to them, and the management is grateful for their patience, their discipline, and their affection for the children.

Our Commitment Going Forward

The ceremony closed with a promise rather than a speech. Uttam Public School will continue to invest in its teachers through training, better classroom resources, and a working culture where good ideas are shared instead of guarded. In return, our faculty continues to give students what no facility can replace: attention, encouragement, and the confidence that someone in the room believes in them.

To every teacher who has taught here since 2004 and to those who joined this year, thank you. The recognition was well deserved, and the best of it will be seen in the students who walk out of our gates ready for whatever comes next.
