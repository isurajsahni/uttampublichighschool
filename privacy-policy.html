Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Uttam Public High School ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of parents, students, staff, and visitors to our website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website or sharing your information with us, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

We only collect information that helps us respond to you and serve our school community. This may include:

  • Contact details you provide through our enquiry and contact forms — such as the student's name, parent or guardian name, email address, and phone number.
  • Admission enquiry details such as the class you are applying for and any message or additional information you choose to share.
  • Technical information collected automatically, such as your browser type, device, and general usage of the site, used only to keep the website working well.

How We Use Your Information

Information you share with us is used to:

  • Respond to admission enquiries, questions, and requests for information.
  • Communicate with parents and guardians about the admission process and school updates.
  • Maintain and improve the content and performance of our website.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences and understand how the site is used. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; please note that some parts of the site may not function as intended if cookies are turned off.

How We Share Information

We treat your information as confidential and share it only when necessary — for example, with school staff handling admissions, or where we are required to do so by law. Any service providers who help us operate the website are expected to protect your information and use it only for the purposes we specify.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us against loss, misuse, and unauthorised access. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

As a school, much of the information we handle relates to children. Information about students is provided by parents or guardians, and we handle it with care and only for legitimate educational and administrative purposes.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites, such as social media pages or map services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies.

Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to ask what personal information we hold about you or your child, to request corrections, or to ask us to remove your details from our records, subject to any legal or record-keeping obligations.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
