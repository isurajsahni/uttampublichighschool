Admissions

Join the Uttam Family

Choosing a school is one of the most important decisions a family makes, and we are glad you are considering us. At Uttam Public School we welcome children from Nursery through Class X into a warm, disciplined environment where every student is known by name.

Admissions are open through the year, subject to seats being available in each class. If you would like to visit the campus, meet our teachers, or simply ask a question before applying, we would be happy to hear from you. The steps below explain exactly how the process works.

The Admission Process

Four simple, unhurried steps — from your first enquiry to your child's first day.

1

Enquiry

Visit or call the school to enquire about available seats for your child's class, and to arrange a look around the campus.

2

Registration

Collect and submit the registration form along with the required documents listed further down this page.

3

Interaction

A short, friendly interaction or assessment with the child — and with parents for the younger classes — so we get to know one another.

4

Confirmation

On selection, complete the admission by paying the fees and submitting any remaining documents. Your child is then all set to begin.

Documents Required

Please keep the following ready when you come in to register. Photocopies are fine, but do bring the originals for verification.

  • Birth certificate of the child (photocopy).
  • Previous school report card or Transfer Certificate (for admission to Class II and above).
  • Four passport-size photographs of the child.
  • Aadhaar card copy of the child and both parents.
  • Address proof (any government-issued document).
